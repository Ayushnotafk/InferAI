## Interpretation

Held-out accuracy (95.5%) is substantially lower than in-distribution random holdout (~99.6%), indicating **distribution shift** between template-heavy training data and curated real-world test examples. Balanced accuracy and macro F1 penalize the skew where some classes (e.g. Upamana) achieve high recall while others (e.g. Shabda, Pratyaksha) trade precision and recall.
